# ChargedLife Pre-Alpha 1.0.57

## Business Overview
- Replaced the previous oversized Business Overview with four focused summary cards:
  - Total Revenue
  - Total Profit
  - Total Employees
  - Total Businesses
- Added a clean list of owned businesses.
- Added a prominent Manage button on the left side of each business row.
- Removed milestones, financial-distribution panels, strategic opportunities and other unrelated sections from this page.

## Per-business management
- Added a dedicated management shell for each selected business.
- Added the following business tabs:
  - Overview
  - Inventory & Supplies
  - Locations
  - Marketing
  - Employees
  - Analytics & Reports
  - Upgrades
- The business Overview reuses the existing detailed restaurant overview.
- Business selection now updates the selected business before opening its management center.

## Inventory & Supplies
- Rebuilt the page into a clearer stock-and-order workflow.
- Added stock summary cards, a compact inventory table, supplier selection, order quantities, discounts, delivery choices and pending deliveries.
- Kept supplier loyalty, contracts, quantity discounts and delivery timing connected to the existing gameplay systems.
