# ChargedLife Pre-Alpha 1.0.58

## Business Overview
- Fixed generic names such as `Fast Food Group` appearing instead of the player-selected company/business name.
- Existing saves with generic placeholder names now use the saved company name automatically.
- Removed the initials square displayed before each business name.
- Increased business names, values, headings, helper text, status labels, and Manage-button sizing.
- Improved row spacing, alignment, hover feedback, and overall readability.

## Business naming
- New companies now use the entered company name as the initial fast-food business name.
- Creating the first restaurant no longer overwrites that business name with a generic restaurant-group name.
