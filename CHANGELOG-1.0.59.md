# ChargedLife Pre-Alpha 1.0.59

## Business overview
- Removed the restaurant exterior/avatar artwork from the business Overview tab.
- Replaced the artwork-heavy panel with a compact operational overview.
- Added clear cards for operational status, employees, inventory, supplier, fixed costs and restaurant value.
- Kept opening progress and management actions visible without taking excessive vertical space.

## Layout and readability
- Increased important text sizes in the business-management header, tabs and overview cards.
- Reduced wasted height so the entire management overview fits more naturally at 100% browser zoom.
- Added compact-height behavior for desktop displays below 920 pixels tall.
- Improved spacing in metrics, calendar, notifications and quick actions.

## Compatibility
- Preserved the existing save key and added migration support from Pre-Alpha 1.0.58.
